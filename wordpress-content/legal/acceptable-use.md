# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy sets out what you may and may not do with northspecs.ca and with the products we supply. It forms part of our Terms of Use. Breach is a material breach of those Terms.

## 1. Permitted use of products

Products may be used only for lawful in-vitro laboratory research by a qualified researcher, laboratory or research institution, in facilities appropriate to the material, and in accordance with applicable law, institutional policy and good laboratory practice.

## 2. Prohibited use of products

You must not:

- administer, ingest, inject, inhale, apply or otherwise introduce any product into the body of a human or an animal;
- supply, resell, gift, distribute, repackage, relabel or compound any product for human or veterinary use;
- supply any product to a person you know or reasonably suspect intends to use it in a human or animal;
- present any product as a drug, supplement, cosmetic or therapy, or make any therapeutic claim about it;
- use any product in a clinical trial, compassionate-use programme or any activity involving living human or animal subjects;
- use any product to produce a controlled substance, weapon or other unlawful material;
- export, re-export or divert any product in breach of trade control, sanctions or export control law;
- remove, obscure or alter any label, hazard marking, batch identifier or documentation.

## 3. Prohibited use of the website

You must not:

- attempt to gain unauthorised access to any account, system, server or network, or probe or test the vulnerability of any of them;
- circumvent authentication, access controls, rate limits or usage restrictions;
- scrape, crawl, harvest, mine or bulk-extract catalogue data, pricing, articles or certificates, or use automated means to place orders;
- introduce malicious code, or take any action that imposes an unreasonable load on our infrastructure;
- interfere with any other user's access to the service;
- impersonate any person, misstate your institutional affiliation, or provide false information in registration, checkout or the research-use attestation;
- use the site to transmit unlawful, defamatory, infringing or misleading content;
- copy, republish or create derivative works from site content except as permitted in the Terms of Use;
- use the site or its content to build, train or evaluate a competing service or a machine-learning model without our prior written consent.

## 4. Accounts

One account per researcher or institutional purchasing contact. Do not share credentials. Do not create an account to evade a suspension or a refused order.

## 5. Reporting

Report suspected misuse, a security vulnerability or a compromised account to compliance@northspecs.ca. Please give us a reasonable opportunity to respond before disclosing a vulnerability publicly.

## 6. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected breaches and may, without notice and without liability, remove content, cancel orders, suspend or close accounts, refuse future business, retain evidence, and report the matter to a regulator or law enforcement. We may disclose information where necessary to investigate a breach of an agreement or a contravention of law, as permitted under applicable privacy legislation.

Breach also engages the indemnity in our Terms of Use.

## 7. Contact

Compliance and misuse reports: compliance@northspecs.ca
