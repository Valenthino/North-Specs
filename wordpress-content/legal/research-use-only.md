You are solely responsible for the safe handling of all products from the moment they are delivered. This includes:

- maintaining appropriate containment, ventilation and personal protective equipment;

Reference material we publish, including reconstitution guidance and the reconstitution calculator, is provided as a laboratory convenience only. It is not a protocol, not a dosing instruction, and not a substitute for your own validated procedures and independent verification.
