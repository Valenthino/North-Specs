## Research Use Only and compliance {#compliance}

### What does "Research Use Only" actually mean?
All products offered by North Specs Labs are intended strictly for laboratory research purposes. These materials are not for human consumption and are not intended for diagnostic, therapeutic, or medical use. They are not drugs, foods, supplements, cosmetics, medical devices or veterinary products, and none has been approved by Health Canada, the FDA, the EMA or any other regulator for use in humans or animals.

### What am I confirming when I place an order?
By purchasing from our website, customers confirm that they are qualified researchers, laboratories, or research institutions and fully understand the research-use-only designation associated with these products. In practical terms you are confirming that you have the training and facilities to handle research chemicals, that the material will be used only for in-vitro laboratory research, and that it will not be administered to any human or animal or resold for that purpose. The full attestation is in our Research Use Only Policy.

### Do I need to be affiliated with an institution?
You need to be a qualified researcher or an authorised representative of a laboratory or research institution. We capture your institutional details at registration and review orders before dispatch. Independent researchers with appropriate training and facilities can order, but we may ask more questions about the intended research application.

### Will you ever refuse an order?
Yes. We review every order before dispatch and we will decline, cancel or refund where we have reasonable grounds to believe material may be intended for human or veterinary use, for resale to consumers, or for any unlawful purpose. We may also request verification of institutional affiliation or limit quantities. A refusal is not a breach of contract and your remedy is a refund.

### Can you advise me on dosing or administration?
No. Our scientific team can help with specifications, purity, solubility, storage, batch documentation and handling. We cannot and will not advise on administration, dosing for living subjects, or any clinical question, and we decline those requests. Supplying you does not create any professional healthcare relationship.

### Is it legal for me to buy these in my country?
You are responsible for determining that. Some materials are restricted or prohibited in some jurisdictions, and you are the importer of record on international orders. We do not give legal advice on import legality. Check your national regulations and your institution's policies before ordering.

### You cite published studies. Does that apply to your material?
No. Citations give scientific context only. A cited study was not conducted with our material, its findings may not be reproducible with our material, and nothing described in it should be expected as an outcome. Study design, model, purity and preparation all vary.

## Quality, purity and documentation {#quality}

### How is purity determined?
By high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), with identity confirmed by mass spectrometry. The certificate of analysis states the method and the result for that specific batch.

### What does "≥99% purity" mean?
It means that under the stated HPLC method and conditions, the target peptide accounts for at least 99% of the integrated peak area. It is a statement about that analytical method on that batch, not a universal claim. Check that the method and specification suit your application.

### Is testing done in-house or independently?
Batches are verified by independent third-party analytical laboratories. The certificate identifies the testing party and the date of analysis.

### Where do I find the certificate for my vial?
Each product page links to the batch certificate, and the full archive is in the Certificates of Analysis section. Match the batch number printed on your vial to the certificate. If you cannot locate it, email research@northspecs.ca with the batch number.

### Does a certificate cover every vial in the order?
It covers the batch identified on it. If your order was filled from more than one batch, the packing list shows which, and each has its own certificate.

### What is the remainder in a ≥99% pure peptide?
Typically residual synthesis-related species, counter-ions such as acetate or trifluoroacetate, and residual water. The certificate reports the analytical detail available for that batch. If your assay is sensitive to counter-ion or residual solvent, ask us before ordering.

### My results do not match the specification. What do I do?
Contact research@northspecs.ca with your analytical data and the batch number. Our scientific team will investigate. Where an independent analysis substantiates a genuine non-conformity we will replace or refund and cover the reasonable cost of that analysis. Note that an unexpected experimental result is not by itself evidence of a defect.

## Handling, storage and reconstitution {#handling}

### How should lyophilised peptides be stored?
Follow the storage condition stated on the product page and certificate. As a general laboratory rule, lyophilised peptides are kept sealed, dry, protected from light and frozen for long-term storage. Allow a vial to reach room temperature before opening to limit condensation.

### How long is lyophilised material stable?
Stability varies by sequence. The product page states the expected shelf life under the stated conditions. Peptides containing cysteine, methionine or tryptophan are generally more susceptible to oxidation and warrant closer attention.

### How should material be handled after reconstitution?
Reconstituted peptide is markedly less stable than lyophilised powder. General laboratory practice is refrigerated storage, protection from light, and aliquoting to avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles. Record the reconstitution date. Follow your own validated procedures.

### What diluent should I use?
That depends on the peptide's solubility and your application. Bacteriostatic water is common for laboratory handling of many research peptides; poorly soluble sequences may need a different approach. The product page notes solubility characteristics, and our team can advise on the chemistry.

### How do I work out the volume to draw?
Use our reconstitution calculator. Enter the vial mass, the diluent volume and your target quantity, and it returns the concentration, the volume, and the exact syringe units, with a live syringe showing the fill. It is a laboratory convenience only; verify every calculation against your own protocol.

### Why does my calculated draw come out below two units?
Because the concentration is high relative to your target quantity. Very small draws are hard to measure accurately. Reconstituting with more diluent lowers the concentration and gives a larger, more readable draw. The calculator flags this.

### Can I avoid repeated freeze-thaw?
Aliquot into single-use volumes immediately after reconstitution and freeze the aliquots. Each freeze-thaw cycle is an opportunity for degradation and aggregation.

## The catalogue {#catalogue}

### What do the research categories mean?
They group compounds by the research area in which they are most studied: metabolic research, growth hormone secretagogues, tissue repair and recovery, longevity and cellular health, neuro and cognitive, and bioregulation and signalling. The grouping is a navigational convenience, not a claim about effects.

### Do you sell blends?
Yes, where a combination is commonly used in published research models. Each component and its mass is stated, and the batch certificate covers the blend as supplied.

### Can you source a compound you do not list?
Sometimes. Email research@northspecs.ca with the sequence or CAS number, the quantity and the purity you need, and we will tell you whether we can source it and on what timeline.

### Do you supply reference standards?
For selected compounds. Ask us with your specification.

### Why do prices differ so much between compounds?
Synthesis difficulty. Length, sequence complexity, difficult couplings, cyclisation and purification burden all drive cost, and a long or awkward sequence can be an order of magnitude more expensive to produce than a short one at the same purity.

You can request deletion, and we will comply where we no longer have a lawful basis to keep the data. We cannot delete order records or the research-use attestation within their statutory retention period. Email privacy@northspecs.ca.

No. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with payment processors, carriers, customs authorities, service providers acting on our instructions, professional advisers, and regulators or law enforcement where legally required.
