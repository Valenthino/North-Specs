# North Specs Labs Small Design Prompt

Use this prompt for a social post, web banner, email header, product tile, announcement card, document cover or other compact brand asset.

Replace every bracketed field before using it.

```text
Create a polished [ASSET TYPE] for North Specs Labs at [WIDTH x HEIGHT OR ASPECT RATIO].

Goal: [ONE CLEAR COMMUNICATION GOAL]
Primary message: [HEADLINE]
Supporting copy: [SHORT SUPPORTING COPY]
Call to action: [CTA, OR "NONE"]
Required product or subject: [PRODUCT, COMPOUND, DOCUMENT OR TOPIC]
Required assets: [ATTACHED VIAL PHOTO, LOGO OR OTHER SOURCE]
Required Research Use Only line: "For laboratory Research Use Only. Not for human or veterinary use."

Follow the North Specs Labs "Forest Assay" design system exactly:
- Warm bone #F7F4ED background with white #FFFFFF surfaces.
- Graphite-green #141F18 primary text, never pure black.
- Forest green #1B573A as the single general accent.
- Soft assay green #D8EEE1 for verified or documented status.
- Amber #B3936C only for a genuine measurement or technical reading.
- Cabinet Grotesk-style bold headings, Public Sans-style body text and JetBrains Mono-style text only for real technical metadata.
- 6px radius for buttons and controls, 12px for cards, full pill only for badges.
- Thin #DDD6C7 borders and very soft green-tinted shadows.
- Generous negative space, disciplined alignment and a premium laboratory-supply feeling.
- Use subtle assay lines or a molecular lattice only when it supports the content.

Composition:
- Create one strong focal point and a clear reading order.
- Keep the headline short and highly legible.
- Use the supplied product image accurately. Do not change the product name, vial strength, label text, logo or packaging.
- Keep all important text inside safe margins.
- Keep the RUO line legible without making it the dominant headline.
- If the asset is small, remove secondary decoration before reducing text size.

Tone: precise, responsible, calm, evidence-led and premium. It must feel like laboratory research supply, not consumer wellness, a supplement advertisement or a medical clinic.

Do not use purple or blue biotech gradients, neon colours, pure black, heavy glow, glassmorphism, excessive pills, fitness imagery, treatment imagery, needles entering skin, before-and-after imagery, medical claims, dosage guidance, invented certifications or invented scientific results.

Deliver a clean production-ready design with accurate text and no placeholder copy. Preserve editable space if requested. Output format: [PNG, JPG, SVG, HTML/CSS OR DESIGN FILE].
```
